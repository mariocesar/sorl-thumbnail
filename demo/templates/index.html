sorl-thumbnail

Thumbnails for Django. Resize, crop and convert images from your templates. sorl-thumbnail makes each thumbnail once, saves it through your Django storage, and gives you its URL.

Features

  • Resize to a box, a width or a height. Crop by position, percentage, pixels or a crop box. See geometry and crop.
  • Output JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF or AVIF, with quality and progressive JPEG options. See formats and quality.
  • Grayscale, padding, rounded corners, blur, border removal and smart crop, depending on the engine. See colorspace, padding and effects.
  • Follows EXIF orientation. Keeps GIF animation with Wand and libvips, and with Pillow after 13.2.0. See orientation and animation.
  • The {% templatetag openblock %} thumbnail {% templatetag closeblock %} tag, the is_portrait and margin filters, and get_thumbnail() for Python code. See the template reference.
  • Reads and writes through any Django storage, including Amazon S3 with django-storages. Thumbnail sizes are cached in a key-value store: the cached database or Redis. See the settings.
  • An ImageField that validates uploads, and an admin mixin that shows a preview. See the model examples.

History

Mikko Hellsing wrote sorl-thumbnail in 2010. Mario-César started contributing in 2013 and maintains it today. From 2017 to 2026 it was part of Jazzband, and it now lives at github.com/mariocesar/sorl-thumbnail. Every release is listed in the changelog.
